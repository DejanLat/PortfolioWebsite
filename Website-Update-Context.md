## PRISM - the product

**Full name:** Periscopic Relay Imaging Scanning Microscope

**What it is:** A precision scanning microscope I designed, built, and iterated. Comparable systems from established vendors cost ~$70,000.

**Current status:** The NPQO Lab at IQC is using PRISM as their working instrument for Diamond NV center research. This is not a pilot or a demo - it's the lab's real tool for sensitive quantum sensing work. The instrument passed from prototype to research-grade through my time embedded at the NPQO Lab.

**Key technical domains:** opto-mechanical design, confocal microscopy, precision scanning, custom software controls (Python, LabVIEW), FEA, CNC machining, rapid prototyping.

**Current active development:** switching structural parts from Al 6061 to PPS-CF10 (3D printed on BambuLab H2D). FEA confirmed PPS-CF10 matches Al 6061 deflection performance. V6 is being designed to be optimized for printability.

## Voice Guide

**Use this tone for all website copy:**

- Builder energy - enthusiastic, direct, technically credible
- Not corporate - no "solutions," "leverage," "synergy," "optimize for impact"
- Not AI-sounding - no filler phrases, no overuse of "passionate about"
- First person is fine
- Short sentences beat long ones
- Specific beats vague - name the lab, name the instrument, name the material

**Examples of good vs. bad:**

Good: "PRISM is in daily use at IQC's NPQO Lab for Diamond NV center research."
Bad: "I am passionate about leveraging cutting-edge instrumentation solutions to advance quantum research."

Good: "I built this from scratch. Multiple iterations, real fabrication, real lab deployment."
Bad: "I have experience in rapid prototyping and iterative design methodologies."
